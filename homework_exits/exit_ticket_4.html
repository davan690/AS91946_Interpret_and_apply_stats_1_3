Teacher Answer Key / Marking Guide

  • Context 1 (Project Kōura):
    • Kōura moving:
      • Type: Measurement Variation/Error.
      • Management: Standardise the process. E.g., wait for the kōura to settle, use a container, or take multiple readings and find the average.
    • Kōura feeding:
      • Type: Natural/Inherent Variation.
      • Management: Take a large and random sample to ensure these natural differences are represented proportionally and don’t bias the overall average.
    • Writing 45g for 54g:
      • Type: Transcribing/Recording Error.
      • Management: Double-check data entry, have a second person verify, and review data for obvious mistakes after collection.
    • Only 4 juveniles:
      • Type: Sampling Variation/Error.
      • Management: Increase the sample size and use a random sampling method to ensure a more representative mix of the population is captured.
  • Context 2 (Extreme Sports):
    • Late stopwatch: Systematic Variation (or systematic error/bias). It consistently makes the recorded times shorter than they actually were.
    • Dry vs. Muddy: Induced Variation (or a confounding/lurking variable). The weather conditions introduce an external factor that systematically affects completion times.
    • Assumptions (any two):
      • Assume the volunteers were trained and used a consistent timing method.
      • Assume the timing equipment was accurate and functioned correctly.
      • Assume any obvious recording errors were identified and corrected.
      • Assume the sample of riders was representative of the intended population.
