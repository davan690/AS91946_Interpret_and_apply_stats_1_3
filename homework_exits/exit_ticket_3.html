Teacher Answer Key / Marking Guide

  • Q1 (Mistakes): 15 and 300.

  • Q2 (Cleaned Dataset): [ 1, 2, 0, 1, 1, 2, 0, 1 ] (order does not matter here).

  • Q3 (Mode): 1 (it appears 4 times, more than any other number).

  • Q4 (Median):

    1. Ordered data: [ 0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2, 2 ]
    2. Median: 1 (the middle is between the 4th and 5th values, which are both 1).

  • Q5 (Mean):

    1. Total: 8 (0+0+1+1+1+1+2+2).
    2. Mean: 1 (8 / 8).

  • Q6 (Impact of Outliers): The Mean would be most affected. The mean uses every value in its calculation, so an extreme outlier like 300 will heavily skew the result, whereas the median is only based on the middle position and is more resistant to outliers.
